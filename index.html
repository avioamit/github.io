Our Temple

Rooted in devotion, guided by compassion.

Shree Tahl Mata Temple is a welcoming spiritual sanctuary for families and individuals seeking peace, purpose, and connection. Through prayer, cultural programs, education, and seva, we nurture a vibrant community that honors timeless traditions and values.

From daily aarti to special festivals, every gathering is designed to inspire reflection, gratitude, and service to all beings.
